Nippli for iPhone

Privacy
policy

Nippli collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Your health data never leaves your iPhone.

The short version

Nippli reads swim workouts and water temperature from Apple Health on your device, draws charts from them, and stops there. Team Dub Labs LTD. never receives that data, because the app has no way to send it.

What Nippli reads

With your permission, Nippli reads two kinds of data from Apple Health on your iPhone:

  • Swim workouts, including their start and end times, duration, distance and swimming location where recorded.
  • Water temperature readings.

Nippli asks for this access the first time you open it. You can grant or refuse it then, and change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Nippli. If you refuse, the app simply shows nothing.

Nippli only reads. It never adds to, edits or deletes anything in Apple Health.

What leaves your device

Nothing.

Nippli has no servers, makes no network connections, and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. Your health data is read from Apple Health, held in memory while the app is open, and never transmitted anywhere. Team Dub Labs LTD. cannot see it.

The only settings Nippli stores are your temperature unit preference and your current filter selection. These live on your device.

Exporting your own data

Nippli can export what you are looking at as a CSV file. That export is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to send it. Once you share it with another app or service, this policy no longer covers it, and that recipient's own privacy terms apply.

Children

Nippli is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because Nippli holds no data of yours, there is nothing for us to delete. Deleting the app removes its stored unit and filter preferences. Your swim workouts and temperature readings belong to Apple Health and are unaffected; manage them in the Health app.

Changes to this policy

If Nippli ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page is updated before that version ships, and the date below changes with it.
