Common questions

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. It's a working conversation, not a sales pitch: you tell us what's slow, manual, or missing, and we tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing. If it isn't, we'll say so, and you've lost nothing but half an hour. Book a call directly, or email us below.

What does discovery cost?

Discovery starts at $2,500, as a fixed price quoted up front before you commit: no hourly meter, no surprises. It ends with a plan you own outright: what to build, how, and what it will cost.

Can we take the plan and build it ourselves?

Absolutely: that's the point of paying for discovery. The plan is yours, so handing it to your own team or another shop is a perfectly good outcome. No hard feelings, no strings.

How long does a project take?

Discovery typically runs two to three weeks. Builds vary too much to promise a number here and mean it, so your discovery plan includes a timeline for your specific project. Small automations can land in weeks; custom software takes longer.

Who owns what we pay for?

You do. The plan, the documentation, and everything built specifically for you is yours on final payment: no licence fees back to us, no lock-in. Where our own pre-built tooling is part of your system, you get a permanent licence to it, so you can run it, change it, or hand it to another developer without ever needing us again.

What happens after handoff?

Every build lands documented and handed over, with a 30-day warranty: anything we got wrong, we fix free. Beyond that, ongoing support is available as a simple monthly arrangement if you want a safety net, and systems running on our infrastructure include monitoring and upkeep as part of the deal.

If you're one person, why do you keep saying "we"?

Because it's honestly not a solo act. Team Dub Labs is Wes plus a roster of AI agents that handle research, drafting, testing, and the operational grind, with Wes directing the work, reviewing everything, and personally accountable for anything that ships. It's the same approach we recommend to clients: one person running well-built AI covers ground that used to take a team.

You're one person. What if you're unavailable?

That risk is designed for, not ignored. Everything is documented from day one so another developer could take over, we build on standard, boring technology rather than anything exotic, and nothing we ship needs us around to keep running.

Is our data safe with AI?

Private by default. Your business data never goes into free consumer AI tools. When cloud AI is part of your build, we use commercial services with contractual no-training terms, and your plan names exactly what touches your data and where. And if nothing can leave your building, we build fully self-hosted AI: we run that stack ourselves, every day.

What happens to my data after an on-site AI job?

Nothing leaves your building. The job runs on our machine, at your site, with no cloud and no internet in the loop. By default we securely erase our drives before we leave, and if your compliance needs more, that's fine too: written confirmation, or keep the storage drive and destroy it yourself. More about On-Site AI.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. If your project needs a confidentiality agreement before we talk specifics, send yours over, or we can provide one.

Do you work outside Regina?

Yes. Anything in person, including training, we do anywhere in Saskatchewan. Project work mostly happens remotely anyway, so for AI, automation, and software builds we work with businesses across Canada.
