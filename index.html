How we work

1 · Talk

It starts with a free conversation. No pitch. Tell us what's slow, what's manual, what's missing. If it isn't worth fixing, we'll say so.

2 · Discover

If it is, we run a paid, fixed-scope discovery and hand you the plan: what to build, how, and what it'll cost. You paid for it, so you own it.

3 · Build

From there it's your call. Take the plan in-house and build it yourself, or we carry on and build it for you, proving it on your real data as we go.

4 · Run

Either path lands the same place: a system running in production, on your infrastructure or ours, documented and handed over.
