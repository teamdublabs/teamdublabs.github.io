Who it's

You have 90,000 files to sort and an agreement that says cloud AI is off the table. Buying and learning the hardware for a one-time job makes no sense. Law firms, clinics, accountants, anyone holding data that can't leave the building: this is for you.

How it works

1 · Scope

A free call. What the files are, what done looks like, whether it's feasible. You get a fixed quote before committing to anything.

2 · Arrive

We show up with an NVIDIA supercomputer. It needs a desk and a power outlet, not a server room, and the internet stays out of the loop.

3 · Work

The job runs at your site: sorting, classifying, extracting, summarizing, whatever was scoped. You see results as they land, not after we're gone.

4 · Leave

Results handed over, machine goes home clean. Nothing uploaded, nothing copied, nothing left behind except your finished output.
